## What it does

Takes one leaf photo and answers: which crop (turmeric or black gram), healthy or diseased, how much of the leaf is affected, and how sure it is. When a check fails it declines to answer instead of guessing (not a leaf of either crop, blurred or dark photo, or more than one label plausible).

## Training data

- Clean TRAIN split of the two lab datasets (turmeric Mendeley g46dvrcvwn, black gram BPLD zfcv9fmrgv) plus background-swap views.
- p4_train: 995 disease-labelled field leaves (blackgram/diseased 716, blackgram/healthy 177, turmeric/diseased 74, turmeric/healthy 28), grouped by capture minute, field scene and near-duplicate so no group crosses p4_train / p4_val / p4_test (data/p4, docs/PLAN_phase4_more_data.md).
- Sources and licences: docs/LICENSES.md (all data CC BY 4.0).

## Selection

32 configurations re-fitted and scored on p4_val (333 leaves). Best point `F_fusion_dinov2[percrop,orig]` (0.987); 24 configurations (it included) scored within its CI, and the pre-registered rule took the fastest of them: `D_dinov2[pooled,orig]` (0.978).

## Intended use and limits

- Research prototype for turmeric and black gram leaves; not a substitute for an agronomist.
- Field data come from a handful of Mendeley datasets (docs/LICENSES.md); wild / wild_ext share sources (not photos, scenes or capture minutes) with p4_train, so they measure new photos from known sources. Results on unseen sources: results/final_report.md (Phase 3).
- Turmeric field leaves with disease labels are few (p4_test n 34); turmeric field numbers carry wide intervals.
- Labels are the dataset authors'; no agronomist re-check.
