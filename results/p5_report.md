## Switch rule

- met: p4_test macro-F1 difference: point >= -0.005 and CI lower bound > -0.02
- met: share of p4_test leaves answered is higher
- met: end-to-end accuracy on answered p4_test leaves >= 0.97
- met: clean TEST macro-F1 drops by no more than 0.02
- met: other-species refusal not lower by more than one leaf

## What this does and does not show

- The Phase 5 bundle answers more held-out field leaves and is at least as accurate on every set. The price, accepted in the plan before any number was seen, is that some of the extra answers are wrong: the Phase 4 bundle answered fewer leaves and got every one of those right.
- p4_test was scored once before, for Phase 4's own switch. Nothing was fitted or selected on it here, but a second read is weaker evidence than a first; Phase 4's once-only numbers stay in results/p4_report.md.
- The remaining refusals come mostly from the crop gate and the OOD gate, which also keep other plants out. Phase 5 did not loosen them.
- Same data limits as Phase 4: field data from a few sources in one country; few disease-labelled turmeric field leaves.
