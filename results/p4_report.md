## Limits

- Phase 4 trains on other images from the same Bangladeshi sources as wild / wild_ext, so those two sets now measure new images from known sources, not new sources; the Phase 3 numbers in final_report.md keep that claim.
- Turmeric field training data are small; turmeric changes carry wide intervals.
- Labels are the dataset authors'; no agronomist re-check.
