## Causes

**(a) Shortcut learning from capture style: **CONFIRMED**.** With the leaf removed (background only) the CNN still names the crop of 0.954 of clean TEST images (majority baseline 0.628); with the background greyed out (leaf only) it scores 1.000 (original 1.000). The mean-colour fill still shows the leaf's silhouette, so a stricter probe hides the leaf's whole bounding box: the surroundings alone still give crop accuracy 0.841. The background alone is therefore enough to predict the crop. The errors in the wild are all of one kind: turmeric FIELD scenes (foliage around the leaf, iNaturalist + Commons) reach crop accuracy 0.618, while single leaves on white from the independent datasets score turmeric 1.000 (n = 55); blackgram 1.000 (n = 70). In training, turmeric is always on white paper and black gram mostly in the field, so foliage surroundings read as black gram. The capture statistics table shows how different the two training datasets are (resolution, brightness, border colour).

**(b) One 4-class softmax entangles crop and disease: inconclusive.** Labelled wild images contain no crop errors, so crop error rates of diseased vs healthy leaves cannot be compared; the separation of crop and disease (P2-B04) is still adopted because it removes the 4-way softmax from the user-facing output.

**(c) Out-of-distribution input with no calibration or abstention: **PARTLY CONFIRMED**.** Crop ECE rises from 0.004 on clean TEST to 0.078 on wild_val; wrong crop predictions on wild images carry mean confidence 0.754 and are shown without any abstention. Disease ECE: 0.013 clean vs 0.070 wild_val.

**(d) Stage 1 assumes one leaf on a contrasting background: **PARTLY CONFIRMED**.** On 40 field scenes the median leaf-mask coverage is 0.264 of the frame (clean TEST 0.380); 0.175 of field scenes yield a mask under 0.15 of the frame. Coverage alone does not show whether the MAIN leaf was chosen; that is checked visually in P2-B06. On the user's image the mask is a central blob with 1 lesion and 5.672% affected.
